# Asset register — licensing and quality gate

No third-party asset is to be imported until its license and source have been checked.

## Required asset groups

| Group | First-slice need | Quality requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen environment | Training kitchen, stainless benches, sinks, extraction, ovens, cooktops, refrigeration | Realistic proportions, PBR materials, consistent scale |
| Human character | One chef/student character | Natural anatomy, believable face and clothing, usable skeleton |
| Character animation | Idle, walk, turn, reach, hold, cut, stir, season, plate | Natural timing and contact with props |
| Food | A small set of recognizable ingredients | Realistic meshes/textures and distinct raw/prepared states |
| Utensils | Knife, cutting board, pan, pot, spoon, plate | Correct scale and hand-grip pivots |
| Audio | Kitchen ambience, chopping, sizzling, handling | Licensed and appropriately recorded |

## Acceptance gate

Reject assets with unclear licensing, missing commercial-use permission, poor topology, visibly incorrect scale, incompatible skeletons, or a visual style that clashes with the realistic kitchen target.

Do not use real-world commercial brand names for fictional in-game shops, suppliers, products, or companies. Fictional commercial identity belongs to the ANIMO brand family (for example ANIMO Foodservice, ANIMO Market, ANIMO Workwear).
